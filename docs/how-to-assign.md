# How To: Assign

Use the Learning Assignment Tool (LAT) for Ongoing Competency 2025 assignments.

1. Navigate to the Menu > Content Management > Learning Assignment Tool
2. Search for **1558** or **Ongoing Competency 2026: TITLE**
3. Click the dropdown and then click the *Copy* button   
![assign1](https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/OngoingCompetency/assign-1.png)
5. Fill out the Title
6. Select your curriculum:
    1. Click the *X* button for the *Cornerstone Content Development Training* placeholder
    2. Click the *Select Training* button
    3. Search for the Curriculum
    4. Check the select box for the curriculum and click the *Select* button
7. Click the *Next* button until you reach the *Users* page
8. Select the department and job groups:
    1. Click the *Select Users* button   
![assign2](https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/OngoingCompetency/assign-2.png)
    2. Click the *Users* dropdown and select the *Group* option
    3. Enter the *Cost Center/Department Code* into the *ID* field, or for a broader search enter the Department Title/Keyword (e.g. "Emergency Room") into the *Search by Title* field
    4. Check the boxes for the appropriate roles and repeat the search as necessary until all areas have been selected
    5. Click the *Select* button  
![assign3](https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/OngoingCompetency/assign-3.png)
9. Review the selected groups to ensure the correct areas are selected
10. Click the *Generate Initial User List* button
11. Review the number of users included in the assignment. If it seems too high you may have selected the wrong groups; click the *Save Draft* link at the bottom of the screen and reach out to LMS@fairview.org for review.  
![assign4](https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/OngoingCompetency/assign-4.png)
12. When satisfied, click the *Next* button
13. Double check the number of assignments seems accurate
14. Click the *Submit* button  
![assign5](https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/OngoingCompetency/assign-5.png)
15. You can review the status of the assignment and progress of completion for learners by clicking on the assignment you just made   
![assign6](https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/OngoingCompetency/assign-6.png)
